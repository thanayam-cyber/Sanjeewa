Safari & Nature Experiences in Thanamalwila

Discover the wildlife, landscapes, history and natural beauty surrounding Wild Hasthi. Your stay includes a memorable game drive, with additional experiences available to arrange during your visit.

01

Lunugamvehera Safari

Explore the wilderness of Lunugamvehera National Park, where elephants, wild buffalo, deer, crocodiles and abundant birdlife can be encountered among forests, grasslands and waterways. A game drive is included with your Wild Hasthi stay.

Game Drive Wildlife
02

Yala National Park

Venture towards the famous Yala wilderness and experience one of Sri Lanka's best-known wildlife destinations, with opportunities to encounter elephants, leopards, sloth bears, crocodiles and diverse birdlife.

Safari Wildlife
03

Udawalawe National Park

Discover the open landscapes of Udawalawe, renowned for its elephant population and rich birdlife. The park offers a rewarding wildlife experience surrounded by beautiful countryside.

Safari Elephants
04

Nature Exploration

Slow down and experience the natural surroundings of Thanamalwila. Discover local plants, birdlife, landscapes and the quieter side of Sri Lanka's wilderness away from the safari tracks.

Nature Birdlife
05

Ganga Dige — Along the River

Follow the rhythm of the river through the countryside. Walk beneath the trees, listen to birdsong, discover life along the water and enjoy peaceful moments surrounded by nature.

Nature River
06

Sithulpawwa & Weheragala

Explore the ancient landscapes of Sithulpawwa and the rugged surroundings of Weheragala. Experience a unique combination of history, culture, rock formations and wilderness in the Yala region.

History Hiking

Fishing Experience

Enjoy a peaceful fishing experience surrounded by the natural beauty of Thanamalwila. Spend time beside the water, experience local fishing traditions and reconnect with the simple pleasures of life close to nature.

Wildlife Etiquette

Travel that respects the wild

Every guest shares this land with its wildlife. These guidelines keep both safe — please follow them on every excursion.

Do not feed, touch, chase, or block wildlife for photographs.

Maintain a respectful distance from natural migration corridors.

Do not litter or make unnecessary noise while on the reserve.

Strictly remain inside safari vehicles unless explicitly cleared by an official wildlife guide.
