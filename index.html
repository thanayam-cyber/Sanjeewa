The wild

Thanamalwila at the edge of something wild.

There is a particular feeling in Sri Lanka's deep south: wide skies, dry forest, distant calls and roads that seem to disappear into green.

Wild Hasthi was imagined around that feeling. Not as a resort that sits beside nature, but as a place that lets you notice it — quietly, comfortably and at your own pace.
